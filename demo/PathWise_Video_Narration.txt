This script follows the recorded 95-second demo. You can play the video silently and narrate it live.

INTRO / LOGIN
"This is PathWise, our group's academic planning prototype. It is designed for students and academic advisors. I'm starting with Maya, a fictional student account."

STUDENT DASHBOARD
"On the dashboard, Maya can see credits completed, remaining requirements, and an estimated graduation term. These are sample records and not actual NSU degree requirements."

REQUIREMENTS AND TIMELINE
"The requirements page separates completed, ready, blocked, and transferred courses. We also check prerequisites before recommending future classes, so the timeline doesn't place a course before the class it depends on."

WHAT IF? PLANNER / VALIDATION
"One feature we wanted to focus on is the What If? planner. I can change Maya's course load to four per semester and prioritize CSC 301. The system recalculates a proposed schedule, but her original 18 of 40 earned credits remain unchanged. If I try to take CSC 490 before its prerequisite, the system shows an error instead of treating it as a valid plan."

ROLES AND ADVISOR VIEW
"A student can view advisor feedback but cannot write advisor notes. When I sign in as Dr. Rivers, the advisor interface lets me leave a recommendation for an assigned student. I can also switch between the students assigned to that advisor. When Maya signs back in, she can see the recommendation."

TESTING / LIMITATIONS / CLOSE
"We included 20 automated unit tests and 16 HTTP workflow checks, covering the academic calculations, login, permissions, and validation. We also corrected a JavaScript issue found during browser testing. For now, the data is fictional, notes reset when the server restarts, and the app is local rather than connected to a university database. Our goal was to show how the core requirements can work together in a usable prototype."
